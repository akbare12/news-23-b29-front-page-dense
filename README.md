One image summing up 23 November 2026's world news: the AI rout that hit Asia
(Kospi −3.3%), Guterres calling for AI regulation at Bridgetown, and the US lead
over China at a record low in the Asia Power Index — plus 28 more stories from
the day, a world map with numbered pins, and a DATE CHECK box separating what
happened on 23 November from what did not.

## How it works

`draw.py` (Pillow) renders the 1024×1024 front page as `entry.png`.
`main.py` (stdlib only) validates the PNG header and copies it to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`.

## Facts

All 31 stories are dated 23 November 2026. Items from other dates (Democrats
reach 258, Sparta II fire, El Chapo Isidro captured, EU–Indonesia pact,
Nigeria boat, ICJ Myanmar ruling) appear only in the red DATE CHECK box as
LEFT OUT. Claims that rest on one side are marked CLAIM; confirmed wire
reporting is marked REPORTED.
